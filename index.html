Services

What we do

Seven kinds of work, often combined in one contract: design it, build it, move the old content across, host it and keep it running.

  1. Government and institutional websites

    Ministries, councils, universities, PSUs

    Websites built to GIGW 3.0 and WCAG 2.1 AA from the first page. English and Hindi are generated from one source, so the two versions never drift apart.

    • GIGW pages and policies
    • English and हिन्दी
    • DBIM and S3WaaS layouts
    • Tenders, recruitment, RTI
    • Support through STQC audit

  2. Design and prototyping

    Before a line of production code

    We build a working prototype first, with real navigation and real content in both languages. You make decisions on pages you can click through and share with your committee.

    • Audit of the current site
    • Site structure and menus
    • Clickable prototype
    • Design system

  3. Web portals and applications

    Forms, records, dashboards

    Admission, recruitment, grievance and tender portals, dashboards and internal tools, with an admin panel your staff can run without calling us.

    • Role-based admin
    • Online applications
    • Search and filters
    • Payment gateway integration
    • Reports and exports

  4. Managed hosting and AMC

    We are your cloud MSP

We set up the servers, run and monitor them, and handle the cloud billing, so one vendor answers for both the website and its hosting. An annual maintenance contract covers content updates and fixes at an agreed response time.

    • MSP on MeitY-empanelled cloud
    • One bill for site and hosting
    • SSL and firewall

    • Uptime monitoring
    • Content updates

Approach

How a project runs

The same six steps whether the client is a ministry or a private company. Steps two to four repeat until the site passes every check.

  1. STEP 1

    Read

    We go through the tender or brief and list every requirement in a compliance sheet.

  2. STEP 2

    Prototype

    A clickable site with real content in both languages, before the full build begins.

  3. STEP 3

    Build

    Accessible pages, an admin panel your staff can use, and a staging link throughout.

  4. STEP 4

    Audit

    axe-core and screen-reader checks, the GIGW 3.0 checklist and a security scan, before the formal audit.

  5. STEP 5

    Launch

    Deployed on MeitY-empanelled cloud that we manage as your MSP, with SSL, backups, monitoring and redirects from old URLs.

  6. STEP 6

    Maintain

    Content updates, patches and monthly reports under an annual maintenance contract.

Standards

What we build to

Indian government websites are audited against these. We check our own work against them before any auditor does.

StandardWhat it coversHow we check it
GIGW 3.0Guidelines for Indian Government Websites: content, policies, navigation, accessibility and securityPage-by-page checklist before handover

DBIMDigital Brand Identity Manual: the shared header, footer, colours and type of government sitesComponents built to the manual's specifications
