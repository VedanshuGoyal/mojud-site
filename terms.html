2. The service

You book a driver for your car in one of three ways:

  • Round Trip: a driver for a number of hours, starting and ending at your pickup.
  • One-Way: a drive from your pickup to one destination.
  • Multi-Stop: a drive with several stops and errands.

Drivers on MOJUD submit their identity, driving licence and bank details to us before they can take rides. The driver drives your car; MOJUD does not provide cars.

3. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code sent by SMS. Keep your phone secure; bookings made from your account are yours.
  • Give your real name and contact details. The driver of your ride sees your name and number.

4. Bookings

  • Book at least 45 minutes before the start time.
  • Nearby drivers can accept your booking until 30 minutes before it starts. If no driver accepts by then, the booking closes as "No driver found" and nothing is charged.
  • When a driver accepts, the app shows their name, number and rating. The driver needs the start code shown in your app to begin the trip.
  • If a driver hands a ride back before it starts, we offer it to other drivers nearby.

5. Fares

The app shows the fare before you confirm a booking. Round Trip fares follow the hourly rates below. One-Way and Multi-Stop fares are estimated from the same rates for the expected drive time, rounded up to whole hours, and the app marks them as estimates.

6. Payment

  • You pay after the trip is completed, in the app, by UPI (any UPI app). Payments are processed by PhonePe's payment gateway; MOJUD never sees your UPI PIN or bank login.
  • MOJUD receives the payment and pays the driver their share.
  • A payment made outside the app (cash, or to a driver's own UPI ID) is between you and the driver, and our refund policy does not cover it.

7. Cancelling

You can cancel free of charge any time before the trip starts. See the refund and cancellation policy.

8. Your car

  • Your car must be roadworthy, insured and carry its valid papers (registration, insurance, PUC).
  • A driver may refuse to drive a car that is unsafe or missing its papers.

9. Conduct and ratings

Treat drivers with respect, and expect the same. After a trip you and the driver can rate each other from 1 to 5 stars. We may suspend an account for abuse, unsafe behaviour, fraud or repeated non-payment.

10. Liability

We work to match you with a verified driver, but we cannot guarantee that a driver accepts every booking. To the extent the law allows, MOJUD is not liable for indirect losses, and our liability for a ride is limited to the fare paid for that ride.
