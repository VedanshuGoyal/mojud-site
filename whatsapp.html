How it works

From signing to first message

Most of the waiting is on Meta's checks, which usually take a few days.

  1. STEP 1

    Verify

    We create your Meta business account and verify it with your documents.

  2. STEP 2

    Register

    Your WhatsApp number is registered, and Meta approves your display name.

  3. STEP 3

    Templates

    We write your first five message templates. Meta approves each one, usually within a day.

  4. STEP 4

    Contacts

    We load your contact list, with only the people who have agreed to receive messages.

  5. STEP 5

    Send

    Your staff send to everyone or to groups, and see who received and read each message.

  6. STEP 6

    Statement

    Each month you get one statement: messages delivered, credit used and credit left.

Good to know

The rules we work by

Some of these are WhatsApp's rules, some are the law, and some are ours.

Official platform only
We use Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. Unofficial bulk senders break WhatsApp's terms, and their numbers get banned.
Consent first
Messages go only to people who agreed to receive them, through a form, a QR code at your office, or by messaging you first. WhatsApp's rules and the DPDP Act 2023 require it.
Delivered messages only
WhatsApp limits how many announcements one person receives per day from all senders. Messages blocked by this limit, or sent to people not on WhatsApp, are not charged.
Meta sets the category
Meta decides whether a template is an announcement or a personal message when it approves it.
Your account, your number
The WhatsApp account, number, Channel and contact list belong to you. You can remove our access at any time.
Prepaid credit
The setup fee is paid before work starts. Message charges are paid in advance as credit and topped up when needed.
Rates follow Meta
Prices are based on Meta's rates as of 10 October 2026. If Meta changes its rates, ours change by the same amount.
